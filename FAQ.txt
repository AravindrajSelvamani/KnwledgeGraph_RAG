Q: What is the Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai Scheme?
A: Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai is a financial assistance scheme managed by the Special Programme Implementation Department of the Government of Tamil Nadu. It provides a monthly grant of Rs. 1,000 directly into the bank accounts of eligible women heads of households.

Q: What are the eligibility criteria and required documents for Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai?
A: To be eligible for Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thogai, the applicant must be a woman aged 21 years or above, residing in Tamil Nadu, with an annual family income below Rs. 2.5 lakh and household electricity consumption under 3,600 units per year. Required documents include an Aadhaar Card, Ration Card (Smart Family Card), Bank Passbook, and Electricity Bill. Applications are processed through special registration camps and the e-Sevai Portal.

Q: What is the Pudhumai Penn Scheme (Moovalur Ramamirtham Ammaiyar Higher Education Assurance Scheme)?
A: The Pudhumai Penn Scheme is managed by the Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department. It provides a monthly financial assistance of Rs. 1,000 to girl students who studied from Class 6 to Class 12 in Tamil Nadu government schools and pursue higher education such as degree, diploma, or ITI courses.

Q: How can students apply for the Pudhumai Penn Scheme and what documents are needed?
A: Eligible girl students can apply online through the Pudhumai Penn Portal via their college nodal officer. Required documents include Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook, Class 10 and Class 12 Mark Sheets, Transfer Certificate from a government school, and College Bonafide Certificate.

Q: What is the Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS)?
A: The Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS) is managed by the Health and Family Welfare Department. It provides cashless medical and surgical treatment up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year at empanelled government and private hospitals in Tamil Nadu.

Q: Who is eligible for CMCHIS and what documents are required?
A: Families residing in Tamil Nadu with an annual family income less than Rs. 1,20,000 are eligible for CMCHIS. Applicants must submit their Smart Family Card (Ration Card), Income Certificate issued by the Village Administrative Officer (VAO), and Aadhaar Card at the District Collectorate Kiosk or e-Sevai Center.

Q: What is the Naan Mudhalvan Scheme?
A: Naan Mudhalvan is a flagship skill development and career guidance scheme managed by the Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation (TNSDC). It provides industry-relevant technical training, coding courses, language skills, and placement support to college and school students across Tamil Nadu. Students can register through the Naan Mudhalvan Portal using their College ID and Aadhaar Card.

Q: What is the Free Bus Travel Scheme for Women (Vidiyal Payanam)?
A: Vidiyal Payanam is a public transport welfare scheme managed by the Transport Department of Tamil Nadu. It provides zero-ticket free travel for all women, transgender persons, and persons with disabilities in ordinary city and town buses operated by Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporations (TNSTC). No prior application is required, though carrying a valid ID proof like an Aadhaar Card is recommended.

Q: What is the Kalaignarin All Village Integrated Agricultural Development Programme (KAVIADP)?
A: KAVIADP is an agricultural welfare scheme managed by the Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Department. It provides free coconut saplings, agricultural equipment kits, micro-irrigation subsidies, and land development support to small and marginal farmers. Farmers can apply via the Uzhavan App or local Agricultural Extension Centres using their Land Ownership Document (Patta/Chitta), Aadhaar Card, and Bank Passbook.

Q: What is the Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme (Mudhalamaicharin Kaalai Unavu Thittam)?
A: The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme is managed by the Social Welfare and Nutritious Meal Programme Department. It provides free, nutritious hot breakfast on all school working days to students studying in Classes 1 to 5 in government primary schools across Tamil Nadu to improve attendance and nutrition.
